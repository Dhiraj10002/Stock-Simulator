# Stock Market Simulator

## Vision

Build a production-level stock market simulator where users can learn and practice stock market trading using virtual money while experiencing real market conditions.

The platform will provide:

- Real-time market data
- Live charts
- Virtual trading
- Portfolio management
- AI Trade Mentor
- News sentiment analysis
- Performance analytics
- Learning-focused experience

---

# Target Users

- Beginners learning stock market
- College students
- Traders who want to practice
- Investors testing strategies
- Anyone who wants to learn trading without risking real money
